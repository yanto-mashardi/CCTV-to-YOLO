# Local validation
Run client main.py and open http://127.0.0.1:8765 on the campus PC. Preview and recording are off by default. Buttons enable 5-minute annotated preview or local MP4 recording. No second YOLO instance, no extra RTSP reader and no upload of video. Preview is limited to 5 FPS; latest-frame inference capped at 10 FPS. Adjust confidence, line and FPS in config.local.json before restarting. Recording is sampled annotated frames at nominal FPS, not a source-timestamp-faithful raw recording; raw synchronized validation recording and ROI calibration UI remain future work.
Archive default: G:\My Drive\DATA KENDARAAN. Create simulation and observed folders; override via CCTV_GDRIVE_ARCHIVE. CSV only, daily closed files, local spool retained. No ETD integration. Measure actual inference FPS and accuracy before field use.
